# Writer brief: Transform the sentence (GRAMMAR_TRANSFORM)
Read COMMON-WRITE.md and PLAN-0.23.md sections A4 and E. Each item gives a correct sentence (or two) and one instruction;
the learner produces the changed sentence, by choosing among 4 or by typing kana.
- Spread the types for the level. N5: neg, past, pastneg, te-join, want, request, noun-mod (adjective + noun), reason.
  N4: plain, polite, potential, volitional, conditional, noun-mod (clause + noun), passive, causative, giving, reason, contrast, nominalize.
  N3 and N2: keigo (to honorific or humble), formal (spoken → written), casual, passive, causative-passive, conditional, pattern
  swaps named in `instr` (for example: Rewrite with 〜ば 〜ほど.), contrast, reason.
- `instr` is plain English and names the target form when the type alone is not enough.
- `to` is the most natural answer; `alt` lists every other fully correct answer a learner could type (other word order,
  kanji vs kana choice is handled by the app: do not list those).
- `wrong`: exactly 3 WELL-FORMED Japanese sentences that do not follow the instruction (wrong tense, wrong form, changed
  meaning). Never ungrammatical strings.
- `en` translates the result. `l` = the lesson id that teaches the form (find it by grepping lesson titles), or omit.
