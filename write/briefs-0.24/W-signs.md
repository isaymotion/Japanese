# Writer brief: signs (js/data/signs.js) or panels (js/data/signs-panels.js)
Follow COMMON-WRITE-24.md; shapes PLAN A3, content PLAN E. Your task names which of the two files is yours.
signs.js: 7 groups, about 110 signs (12–20 per group), each the text as really printed on signs in Japan (short; real
signs use kanji compounds: keep them, the reading goes in 《》). `en` = what it means to the reader ("No entry", "Out of
order"). `note` only when useful (where you see it, what to do, a look-alike). `lv` = N5, N4 or N3 by the difficulty
of the word for a learner (most sign kanji are N4–N3; that is fine). The medicine-bag group has label words only.
signs-panels.js: 10 panels (PLAN E). Use the wording printed on typical Japanese machines (電源, 入 / 切, スタート, 一時停止,
洗い, すすぎ, 脱水, 冷房, 暖房, 除湿, 温度, 風量, 運転切換, 止, おしり, ビデ, 流す 大 / 小, お預け入れ, お引き出し, 残高照会, 取消, 確認,
あたため, 解凍, 炊飯, 保温, 予約, 開, 閉, 呼出, おつり, 売切 …), generic, no brand names. Rows as they would sit on the device.
5–6 tasks per panel in plain English ("Make the room cooler", "Stop the water"), each with ONE right control and a
one-line `why`. The bed and nurse-call panel: call button, bed up / down, light; tasks include calling the nurse.
