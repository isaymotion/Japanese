# Task B: listening practice page
Read COMMON-CODE.md (same folder) first. Your files: `js/listen.js` (replace the stub), `css/style.css` (append only).

`#/listen` (V.listen(el, p, q)): heading "Listening practice", four tabs (`?tab=`): **Hear** (default), **Dictation** (`dict`), **Sound pairs** (`pairs`), **Dialogues and readings** (`story`). A short muted line under the heading per tab. Common controls where they apply: level seg (Starter, N5, N4, N3; remember in App.state.settings.listenLv), speed seg (Slower 0.7 / Natural 0.9 / Fast 1.05 as in js/point-listen.js V.psychListen, which is the model for this page), "New set" button. onDone: App.activity(10), best % in App.state.done.listen2[tab+':'+variant] (create if missing), App.save().
1. **Hear**: source seg Words / Sentences. Words: dictionary entries (App.DICT) of the chosen level (Starter level = words with lv 'N5' and kana-only… if there is no Starter level in the dictionary, use N5 for Starter) without ＿ and with at most 2 spaces; App.runQuiz with hideQ: 'Listen, then choose the meaning.' (see how hideQ text is used in views.js), say: e.tl, rate, options = English of words from the same category (fallback same level), never two equal English, cardId: e.id, exp = word with furigana + English. Sentences: lesson examples parsed from `window.LESSONS` bodies (`<div class="ex"><b>JA</b><span>EN</span></div>`; the lesson's `level`) plus `window.DICT_EX` sentences (level = the word's level); skip any with ＿ or empty English; options = English of other sentences of the same level (prefer similar length), exp = the sentence + English.
2. **Dictation**: hear a word, build it from kana tiles. Words: dictionary entries of the level whose whole headword reading is 2 to 7 kana, no spaces; the answer is `e.kn` (hiragana), or katakana if the headword is all katakana (then tiles are katakana). Tiles = every character of the answer (small ゃゅょっ and ー are separate tiles) plus 4 to 6 distractor tiles chosen to be confusable with characters in the answer (voiced/unvoiced か/が, small/large や/ゃ, つ/っ, extra う or ー for long vowels, ん, similar shapes like ぬ/め, シ/ツ, ソ/ン), shuffled. Tap a tile to add it, tap the built answer's last char or a ⌫ button to remove, "Check". Also a text box: accepts hiragana, katakana or romaji (compare romaji with `JA.romaji` of the answer, normalised: lower case, no spaces, ou/oo/ō accepted the same, n' accepted). Play again button; after checking show right/wrong with the correct kana, the word with furigana, the English, and play it. Wrong → App.recordAnswer(false, e.id). 10 words a set; score at the end.
3. **Sound pairs**: `window.SOUND_PAIRS` (shape in js/data/sound-pairs.js header). Type seg built from `guide` (+ "All"), with the explanation of the chosen type shown in a card. Each item: play a or b at random; two big buttons with the two words (notation, so furigana shows) and their English; choose which you heard. After answering: right/wrong, both words with ▶ buttons to compare. 10 items a set (fewer if the type has fewer). For testing beyond the one seed item, use /home/claude/work/new/sound-pairs.js if it exists (load it into the page with page.add_script_tag before navigating to the tab) or inject a few items with page.evaluate.
4. **Dialogues and readings**: `window.LISTEN_Q` keyed by dialogue id (`window.DIALOGUES`, `{ id, level, title, lines:[[speaker, ja, en]] }`) or reading id (`window.READING`; look at js/views.js V.passage to see its fields and how its text is spoken). Without `?id=`: list (grouped Dialogues / Readings, level pill, title, number of questions, best score) of the items that have questions. With `?id=`: title, "Play all" (speaks each line/sentence in turn, waiting for each to finish: look at how V.dialogue or V.passage plays all, and reuse that mechanism; also a Stop button), "Play line by line" is not needed; the text is hidden behind "Show the text" (shows the lines with furigana and their English, each with a say button). Then the questions with App.runQuiz (English questions and options; exp = why). A link back to the dialogue/reading page. For testing use /home/claude/work/new/listen-questions.js if it exists, else inject an object with page.evaluate.
