What was changed: the stroke paths and stroke-number positions of the kana and of the 719 kanji taught in
Hanashimashō were copied out of KanjiVG's SVG files, coordinates were rounded to one decimal place, and the
result was stored as JavaScript text (`build-strokes.py` in this folder does it). Stroke shapes and stroke
order are KanjiVG's and were not edited.
